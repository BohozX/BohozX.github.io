Research

My research

Working paper · 2026

USDT Price Dynamics in Bolivia amid Episodes of Domestic Uncertainty, 2023–2026

Examines whether the effect of exchange-rate uncertainty and social conflict on the USDT price changed between July 2023 and June 2026. Using daily data and TVP-SV and eGARCH models estimated over rolling windows, it finds that conflict initially pushed the USDT price up, but that from June 2025 the relationship weakened and then reversed.

Undergraduate thesis · 2026

Optimal Monetary Policy in the Face of External Imbalances in Bolivia, 2011-2025

Assesses which monetary policy would have best addressed Bolivia’s external imbalances between 2011 and 2025. Using a DSGE model estimated with Bayesian techniques, it compares monetary and exchange-rate regimes under external shocks and concludes that a hybrid regime of inflation targeting with a managed float performs best.
